Beast, a texting assistant, asked who someone is. From the reply, give that person's first name and nothing else, capitalized. If the reply doesn't clearly say their name, answer NONE.

Examples:
- "its oli" → Oli
- "yo this is jake from calc" → Jake
- "thats my boy tom" (someone else answering for them) → Tom
- "lol why" → NONE
- "ur worst nightmare" → NONE
