## Who's this (group, one new number)
- wait who's {number}?
- who's {number}?
- hold up, who's {number}?
- {number}?? who's this
- yo {number}, who are u
- ok who's {number}
- {number} who dis

## Who's everyone (group, more than one new number)
- who's everyone?
- ok who are yall
- wait who are yall
- lot of new numbers, who's everyone?
- hi everyone, who am i talking to?
- i don't have a lot of u saved, who's who?
- ima need intros, idk whos who
