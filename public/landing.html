Beast is a school sidekick that knows your classes, what's due, and what you've got going on. It lives in your texts.

There's nothing new to learn. You text it like you'd text a friend. It reads your Canvas, your syllabi and your class websites, so it already knows the stuff you'd otherwise have to dig for.

Beast sends a quick rundown in the morning, nudges you before deadlines sneak up, finds the Zoom link five minutes before lecture, and plans your week around work, practice and everything else you've got.
