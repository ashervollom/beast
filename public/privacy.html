Privacy, in plain words

Beast is an early test run by one student for a small group of invited friends. Here's exactly what it does with your stuff.

What Beast stores

  • Your phone number, name and school.
  • Your assignments, courses and Canvas calendar events.
  • Your last ~30 messages with Beast in each chat, so it remembers the conversation.
  • Things Beast learns about you to help (like your work schedule). Text what do you know about me to see them, or forget one.
  • How often each feature gets used and what it costs to run, so the builder can tell what's worth keeping.

Connections (all optional)

  • Canvas: your access token is stored encrypted and only used to read Canvas. Beast never submits, edits or deletes anything there. Your school's IT hasn't reviewed Beast, so connecting it is your choice, and you can revoke the token in Canvas anytime.
  • Google (if you connect it later): Gmail read access technically covers your whole inbox. Beast's code only looks at mail from your school, Canvas, Ed and Gradescope and ignores the rest.

Who sees it

  • Your dashboard link is private to you. Anyone you send it to can see your board, so text new dashboard link if it gets out.
  • In group chats Beast never shares your private links, email or grades.
  • Messages are processed by Anthropic's Claude to write replies, and sent through Linq to reach iMessage.
  • Nobody's data is sold or shared with anyone else.
  • Signing in on the website uses a one-time code Beast texts you and a sign-in cookie that lasts 30 days. There are no ads or trackers on the site.

Deleting it

  • Text disconnect canvas to delete your Canvas token.
  • Text delete my data, or use Preferences → Delete account on the website, to erase everything Beast has on you, including from its backups.
  • Preferences → Export my data downloads everything Beast has on you.
  • Backups are kept for up to 14 days, then deleted.
